# Liberty Immortal 0.8 beta

**GTA IV on Android, optimized for a stable 30 FPS on Immortalis GPUs.**

Signed with the same key as every earlier Mali / Immortal-Fix build, so it installs over them as an update.
Built for ARMv9 (Dimensity 8400 and newer). Needs the Xbox 360 GTA IV disc image and Title Update 8; the episodes
are optional (see the README).

## Performance
- **Locked 30 FPS** with the screen refresh rate matched to the frame limit (30 on 60 Hz, 40 on 120 Hz).
- **Tuned Mali-G1 / Immortalis profile**, applied once by the app on first launch.
- **Shadow draw budget**: the sun shadow pass is over a third of the draws in a busy street. A frame over 3,200
  draws skips only its smallest shadow casters, slowly, so shadows stay steady and cars and buildings are never
  touched.
- **Farther world**: draw distance 0.8 with a floor of 0.9 (props no longer pop in next to you).
- **Heat guard** trims the draw budget in four stages (x0.95 to x0.80) as the phone heats up, instead of shortening
  the draw distance, and restores it as it cools.
- **Clean sound**: bigger audio device buffer and a shorter game audio queue.
- **Sharper picture**: the base resolution follows the output setting, with SMAA.

## Less memory: ASTC textures
- **Opaque DXT1** to ASTC 4x4: half the memory.
- **DXT3 / DXT5 with simple alpha** (fences, leaves, short gradients) to ASTC: a quarter of the RGBA8 size, with a
  least squares alpha fit. Textures with detailed alpha stay RGBA8 on their own.
- **DXN normal maps** to ASTC luminance + alpha: half the memory of R8G8.
- All transcoders are tested against ARM's reference decoder. Switches if a texture looks wrong:
  `--gta4_native_astc_dxt1=false`, `--gta4_native_astc_alpha=false`, `--gta4_native_astc_dxn=false` in `args.txt`.

## Touch controls
- **Swipe to look** with flick compression, a camera speed slider (hold Pause for 1.5 s) and faster turning while
  you walk or steer.
- **Steering**: the finger that landed last decides the side, steering builds up while held (a tap steers a little,
  a hold goes to full lock), and a thumb on a button's edge does not flip sides.
- **Fewer accidental presses**: drive-by needs 0.15 s, touches at the very edge of the screen are ignored.
- **Menu and map controls**: touch controls for the episode picker, and a left stick, LT / RT zoom and a Y legend
  button on the map and menu screens.
- Layouts for on foot, driving, phone, pause menu and cutscenes, with drag to move and pinch to resize; switch
  to a classic Xbox controller any time.

## Episodes and cheats
- *The Lost and Damned* and *The Ballad of Gay Tony* start from the episode picker. Episode vehicles only spawn
  inside their own episode and boats are placed on water.
- **32 cheat buttons** (CH button): health and armor, ammo, weapon sets, wanted level, weather, base game, TLAD and
  TBoGT vehicles, all instant. Parachute, Explosive punches, Explosive sniper and the advanced weapon set dial the
  phone code the first time and are instant afterwards (the app learns the game's cheat number,
  `liberty_cheat_ids.txt`).

## Debug logs (Play (Debug Log), saved to Downloads/LibertyRecomp)
- Frame-time percentiles, thread load, temperature and thermal headroom, the heat guard stage.
- Draw census, shadow budget and texture statistics (`draw-budget:`, `astc-*:`, `texture-capture:`).
- A CPU sampler (`cpu-thread:`, `cpu-hot:`) that shows where the CPU time and heat go.
- A freeze watchdog (thread stacks after 4 s without a frame), finger events for ghost touch hunting, and a 40 s
  recording of the audio output.

## Known issues
- Beta: expect rough edges and save often.
- Long, hot sessions still warm the phone up and it throttles at thermal status 3; the CPU sampler is how that is
  being measured and reduced.
- Some textures may look different with ASTC (see the switches above); please report them with a log.
- Details of every setting: `docs/android/IMMORTAL_FIX.md`.
